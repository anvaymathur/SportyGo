Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

SportyGo is operated by SparkPro ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the SportyGo app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email contactus@sparkpro.ca.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and date of birth, plus an optional phone number and profile photo. Your email address comes from the account you sign in with.
  • Group and event information: groups you create or join (name, description, skill level, home court, schedule, photo), events you create (title, date, location, cost), your RSVP responses and attendance check-ins.
  • Match results: matches you record, including players, scores and dates.
  • Players you add: when you record a match with someone who doesn't use SportyGo, you can add them as a temporary player with a name and, optionally, their email or phone number so they can claim their match history if they join later.

We use your date of birth only to confirm you are at least 13 years old. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.

How we use information

  • To create and run your account and sign you in.
  • To show your groups, events, RSVPs, attendance and match history to you and to the members of groups and events you take part in.
  • To let people claim temporary players that match their email address or phone number.
  • To respond to support requests and reports, and to keep the service safe.

We do not sell your personal information, show ads, or use your information to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who can see your information

Your name and profile photo are visible to members of groups you belong to and participants in events and matches you take part in. Group members can also see the email address and phone number on your profile. Information you add to a group or event is visible to that group or event's members.

Service providers

We use these providers to run SportyGo. They process information on our behalf:

  • Auth0 (Okta) for sign-in and account authentication.
  • Google Firebase for storing app data and hosting our website.
  • Expo for delivering app updates.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app. Deleting your account removes your profile, removes you from your groups and events, deletes events you created and groups you own that have no other members (groups with other members are handed to another member), deletes your RSVPs and temporary players only you use, and replaces your name in other people's match history with "Deleted player". You can also request deletion by email; see how to delete your account.

Children

SportyGo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices

You can view and edit your profile in the app at any time, leave groups, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your information. Contact us to exercise them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes we will let you know in the app or by updating the date above.
