Delete your SportyGo account

In the app

  1. Open SportyGo and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you can't access the app, email contactus@sparkpro.ca from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my SportyGo account". We'll confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, date of birth and photo.
  • Your membership in groups and events, and your RSVPs.
  • Groups you own with no other members, and events you created. Groups you own that have other members are handed to another member.
  • Temporary players you added that no one else uses.

What we keep

Match results you played with other people stay in their history so their records remain accurate, but your name is replaced with "Deleted player" and the result is no longer linked to you.
